Gunshots
---------
The Free Firearm Sound Library, released under CC0 / No Rights Reserved:
https://opengameart.org/content/the-free-firearm-sound-library
The library was created by Ben Jaszczak, Brian Nelson, Kevin Heras, and Matthew Nanney.

Included excerpts:
- gunshot-pistol.wav: 1911, A_42P.wav
- gunshot-shotgun.wav: Charles Daly, H_21P.wav
- gunshot-rifle.wav: AR-15, D_32P.wav
- gunshot-sniper.wav: Tikka, W_24P.wav
- gunshot-heavy-sniper.wav: Springfield 1917, B_24P.wav

The additional per-weapon shots are CC0 excerpts from the USC / Sunset Editorial Sound Effects Library:
https://archive.org/details/SSE_Library_GUNS

- gunshot-revolver.wav: "Big revolver shots"
- gunshot-pocket-shotgun.wav: "Classic western shotgun blast with reverb"
- gunshot-battle-rifle.wav: "30 30 rifle shots"
- gunshot-smg.wav: "Tommy gun busts; machine gun"
- gunshot-marksman-rifle.wav: "Very sharp small rifle fire"
- gunshot-suppressed.ogg: "FN FNX .45 Tactical (SilencerCo Osprey)" by areniporgen
  (Freesound 693855), recorded close outdoors; CC0 1.0.
  https://freesound.org/people/areniporgen/sounds/693855/

The excerpts were converted to mono 24 kHz PCM and trimmed to short gameplay shots.
The battle pistol uses the classic pistol recording at a lower volume; the assault and
battle rifles share the 30 30 rifle recording; the SMG and machine pistol use the classic
pistol recording; and the machine gun uses the former SMG recording.

Gadget effects

The following excerpts are from the Sunset Editorial / USC Sound Effects Library.
The library items are released under CC0 1.0 Universal:
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

- molotov-ignite.wav: short glass-break excerpt from "Small glass breaks" (GLASS),
  layered with a brief excerpt of "Igniting gas jets; trike and gas flame" (FIRE)
- orbital-strike-fire.wav: 1.2-second fire-only excerpt from "Igniting gas jets; trike and
  gas flame" (FIRE), with no glass-break layer
- smoke-deploy.wav: short excerpt of "Carbon dioxide fire extinguisher; long blast" (AIR)
- flashbang.wav: short excerpt of "Grenade explodes" (EXPLOSIONS), layered with
  a generated, fading high-frequency ring
- Jammer static burst: procedurally synthesized white noise generated with the Web Audio API;
  no external sample
