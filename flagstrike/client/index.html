    Gameplay guide

    5v5

    A tactical shooter that's more tactical than shooter

    Five players fight on each side: the Strike Team attacks and the Flag Team defends. Each three-minute round can be won either by eliminating the opposing team, or capturing/defending the flag. Teamplay, positioning, utility, and timing is more important than mechanical skill.

    WIN CONDITION 01

    Team elimination

    Eliminate every living opponent to win immediately. There are no respawns: when you are eliminated, you spectate until the next round.

    Every player starts with 100 health. Armor absorbs incoming damage before health, and regenerates over time. Your armor depends on the game mode and the gear you have acquired.

    WIN CONDITION 02

    Capture/Defend the flag

    Strike Team: find and pick up the flag at the Flag Site, then carry it into the Strike Site to capture it. The flag carrier moves more slowly; if eliminated, the flag drops and another Strike player can pick it up.

    Flag Team: protect the flag and deny the capture. Eliminate all Strike players to win at once, or keep the flag out of the Strike Site until the three-minute timer expires.

    COMBAT

    How to shoot and where to aim

    Use the left mouse button to fire. Weapons differ in damage, fire rate, effective range, and more. A hit is strongest near the target’s center and weaker toward the edge of their body.

    Accuracy also changes during a fight: movement makes shots less precise, firing adds recoil, and accuracy recovers when you stop shooting.

    Choose a weapon for the role you play

    Some weapons are generally stronger than others, but each weapon is deadly in its circumstances.

    Reload behavior varies too: some weapons replace a magazine, while weapons such as the Revolver load rounds one at a time. Press R to reload.

    Gadgets

    Choose one gadget for each match. Most gadgets have one use; unique gadgets can have special charge and recharge rules.

    • Smoke grenade: A reliable vision-blocking gadget that can be used to create positive cover for your team, and negative intel for the enemy team.
    • Molotov: A fire-based aggressive area denial gadget that creates a pool of damaging fire briefly.
    • Cryo grenade: An ice-based grenade that creates a long lasting slowing field.
    • Proximity mine: A small explosive mine with a detection radius that can cover flanks, and get kills on careless enemies.
    • Flash grenade: A thrown flashbang that blinds and deafens all players within line of sight of the grenade, after a short delay.
    CONTROLS

    All the controls

    • W/A/S/D to move; arrow keys work too.
    • Q / E to lean left / right. Hold to lean; a quick tap toggles the lean on or off.
    • R to reload your weapon.
    • Shift to walk more slowly for controlled movement.
    • F to drop the flag when you are carrying it.
    • Left mouse button to fire. Aim with your mouse. Hold for weapons that support continuous fire; semi-automatic weapons fire once per click.
    • Right mouse button to switch between your weapon and gadget; switching takes 1 second. Right-click again to use the selected gadget. Left-click returns to your weapon.
    • Middle mouse button to ping the aimed map location; teammates see the marker briefly.

    Lean to peek around obstacles without exposing your whole body. Use pings to quickly call out to your team.

    BUY PHASE · PRACTICE

    Pick a weapon before the round

    Hosts can configure custom rooms with no buy phase, a free loadout, or a progression shop. Free loadouts let players choose any available weapon and one gadget before the round.

    BUY PHASE · QUICK PLAY/LONG PLAY

    Plan your picks across the series

    During each pre-round countdown, browse randomized offers. You get 6 offers and 2 picks in the first round, then 5 offers and 1 pick in every later round. You start with the Classic pistol and no gadget.

    Buying a weapon replaces your current one, and buying a gadget replaces your current gadget. Purchased items persist between rounds.

    GLOBAL INTEL SYSTEM

    Vision and sound are shared teamwide

    Your living teammates’ vision provide global intel to everyone on your team, and explored map intel stays known. Enemy positions are shown only while an enemy is currently visible to your team.

    If an unseen opponent makes noise, your team may receive a short-lived sound cue. Orange marks gunfire and red marks footsteps from a nearby moving enemy. Each ring is an uncertain search area. Slow-walking (Shift) prevents audio cues.

    THE BATTLEFIELD

    Maps are procedurally generated

    Each match map is procedurally assembled from a seed: connected rooms and corridors link the Strike and Flag sites, with destructible objects placed throughout.
